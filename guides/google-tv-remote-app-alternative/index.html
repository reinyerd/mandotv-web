Google TV Remote App Alternative for Your Phone

The phone remote for Google TV and Android TV lives inside Google's own app and is tied to your Google account. When it cannot find the TV, you are stuck. Mando TV is a separate remote app that pairs with the 6-character code on the TV screen and works without a Google account, from an iPhone, an Android phone or a Windows PC.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Turn on the TV and connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Download Mando TV and open it. Tap your Google TV or Android TV in the list.
  3. The TV shows a 6-character code. Type it into Mando TV and tap Pair.
  4. Use the d-pad, OK, Back, Home, volume and the keyboard. There is nothing to sign in to.
  5. Optional: look for Wake on LAN or "Turn on over network" in the TV settings so the app can also turn the TV on.

Why a phone remote stops finding the TV

Most of the time it is the network: the phone and the TV are on different Wi-Fi networks, a VPN is on, or a guest network keeps devices apart. Fix that first, because it affects every remote app. See your phone cannot find the TV.

Which TVs it works with

Google TV and Android TV sets from Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips, Sharp, Xiaomi, Toshiba and more, plus Chromecast with Google TV, Google TV Streamer and Nvidia Shield. They all pair the same way, with the code on the screen, once.

Where Google's app is better

Google's app also helps you find what to watch and keeps your watchlist. Keep it for that. Mando TV is just the remote, and it also controls Samsung, LG, Roku and Fire TV from the same app, and from a Windows PC.

If the old Android TV remote app is gone

Google moved its phone remote from the old Android TV Remote Control app into the Google TV app. If you want a standalone remote instead, Mando TV is one.

Free and Pro

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Google account to use Mando TV with Google TV?

No. The code on the TV screen is all it takes to pair.

Does it work with Chromecast with Google TV?

Yes. It pairs the same way, with the 6-character code on the screen.

Can I use it from an iPhone?

Yes. Mando TV is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, on Microsoft Store for Windows, and in free early access on Google Play for Android.
