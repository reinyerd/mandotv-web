Sharp TV Remote App for Your Phone

In the US, Sharp smart TVs run Roku TV. In many other countries, Sharp Aquos TVs run Google TV or Android TV. Mando TV controls both from your phone or PC over your home Wi-Fi, with no account and no ads.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Check the home screen. Roku TV shows a grid of tiles with the Roku menu. Google TV and Android TV show rows of recommendations.
  2. Connect your phone and the TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  3. Open Mando TV and tap your Sharp in the list.
  4. A Roku TV asks for nothing. Google TV and Android TV show a 6-character code: type it into the app once.
  5. Use the d-pad, OK, Back, Home, volume and keyboard.

Sharp Roku TV

Works like any Roku TV, including Sharp's OLED Roku TV models. To turn it on from your phone, turn on "Fast TV start" under Settings, System, Power. More on the Roku remote app page.

Sharp Aquos with Google TV or Android TV

Pairs with the code on the screen. To turn it on from your phone, look for Wake on LAN or "Turn on over network" in the network or power settings. Some models do not have it. More on the Android TV remote app page.

Sharp TVs with another system

Sharp TVs without Roku TV, Google TV or Android TV are not controlled by Mando TV today.

Frequently asked questions

Does Mando TV work with Sharp TVs?

Yes, with Sharp TVs that run Roku TV, Google TV or Android TV.

My Sharp Roku TV ignores the app. What do I do?

On the TV, go to Settings, System, Advanced system settings, Control by mobile apps, and allow network access. The guide [Roku remote app not working](/guides/roku-remote-app-not-working/) has the details.

Do I need an account?

No. Mando TV connects over your home Wi-Fi and has no account.
