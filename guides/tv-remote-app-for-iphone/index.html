TV Remote App for iPhone: One Remote for Every TV

Mando TV turns your iPhone or iPad into a TV remote for Samsung, LG, Roku, Google TV or Android TV, and Fire TV, over your home Wi-Fi. It is free on the App Store, with no account and no ads. It also works on Android and Windows.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Download Mando TV free from the App Store.
  2. Connect your iPhone and the TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  3. Open Mando TV and tap Allow when iOS asks for Local Network access. The app needs it to find the TV.
  4. Tap your TV in the list. Accept the prompt on the TV screen if it asks (LG and Samsung), or type the code the TV shows (Google TV or Fire TV).
  5. Use the buttons, volume and the keyboard. You are connected.

Works with these TVs

LG webOS, Samsung Tizen (2016 and newer), Roku TV and Roku players, Google TV and Android TV, and Fire TV. The brand pages are Samsung, LG, Roku and Android TV.

Why it asks for Local Network

iPhone blocks apps from seeing devices on your home network unless you allow it. If the TV is missing from the list, open Settings, Mando TV and turn on Local Network. The app only talks to your TV inside your home network.

Why an iPhone and not the TV maker's app

Each TV brand has its own app, and each wants its own setup or account. Mando TV is one app for all of them, with no account.

Free and Pro on iPhone

The arrows, OK, Back, Home, volume, channels, number keys, keyboard, opening apps, switching inputs and power on or off are free. The touchpad, voice commands, favorite apps, routines and the sleep timer are part of Mando TV Pro, a one-time purchase with no subscription. Pro is bought once inside the app, with no subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Is Mando TV free on iPhone?

The download is free, with no ads. The basic remote is free, and so are opening apps and switching inputs. Extras such as the touchpad, voice commands and routines are Mando TV Pro, a one-time purchase.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. The same app runs on iPhone and iPad.

Does an iPhone remote app need Bluetooth or an infrared blaster?

Not on iPhone. iPhones have no infrared blaster, so infrared control is not available there, and the Bluetooth option is only for Android 9 or newer, not for iPhone. On iPhone, Mando TV controls smart TVs, and Apple TV in beta, over your home Wi-Fi.
