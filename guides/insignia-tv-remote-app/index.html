Insignia TV Remote App for Your Phone

Most Insignia smart TVs sold today have Fire TV built in, and some earlier Insignia models run Roku TV. Mando TV controls both over your home Wi-Fi, from an iPhone, an Android phone or a Windows PC, with no account and no ads.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Look at the home screen to see which system your Insignia runs. Fire TV shows rows of apps and Amazon content. Roku TV shows a grid of tiles with the Roku menu on the left.
  2. Connect your phone and the TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  3. Open Mando TV and tap your Insignia in the list.
  4. On a Fire TV model, type the 4-digit PIN the TV shows. A Roku TV model asks for nothing.
  5. Use the d-pad, OK, Back, Home, volume and keyboard from your phone.

Insignia with Fire TV built in

The TV is the Fire TV, so the Fire TV remote app guide applies. Fire TV support arrived recently in Mando TV, so update the app first, and tell us from the Help page if your model does not behave. Lost the remote? See lost your Fire TV remote.

Insignia with Roku TV

It works like any Roku TV, with no code. To turn it on from your phone, turn on "Fast TV start" under Settings, System, Power. Details on the Roku remote app page.

Turn on an Insignia without the remote

The power button is usually under the center of the screen or on the back near a lower corner. See how to turn on a TV without the remote.

Insignia TVs that are not smart TVs

An Insignia without Fire TV or Roku TV cannot be controlled over Wi-Fi. Mando TV has infrared control for Android phones with an infrared blaster, but Insignia is not in its list of brand code sets. The "Other brand or I don't know" option tries every code set, so whether it works depends on the model.

Frequently asked questions

Does Mando TV work with Insignia Fire TV models?

Yes. It pairs with the 4-digit PIN on the screen. Fire TV support is new, so keep the app updated.

Can my phone turn on an Insignia TV?

On Roku TV models, yes, once "Fast TV start" is on. On Fire TV models, turn the TV on with its button and then use your phone.

Do I need the Amazon Fire TV app?

No. Mando TV connects over your Wi-Fi and needs no Amazon account on the phone.
