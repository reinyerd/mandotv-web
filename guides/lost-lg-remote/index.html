Lost Your LG TV Remote? How to Use Your Phone

If you lost your LG TV remote or the Magic Remote stopped working, your phone replaces it over Wi-Fi. It works on LG TVs with webOS, which is almost every LG smart TV since 2014. You do not need an LG account or the ThinQ app.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Turn on the TV with the button at the bottom, under the LG logo, or the one on the back. On many models it works as a joystick: press it to open the menu and move it to choose.
  2. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi network as the TV.
  3. Open Mando TV and tap your LG TV in the list.
  4. The TV shows a prompt asking for permission. Accept it with the joystick or the button on the TV. If you connected that phone before, it does not ask again.
  5. Done. The arrows, OK, Back, Home, volume, channels and keyboard are free. The pointer is a Pro feature.
  6. With the app connected, go into Settings with the arrows and OK, and turn on "TV On With Mobile". The guide How to turn on an LG TV with your phone shows where it is.

If the TV does not show the prompt

The permission may already be saved, or your phone may be on a different network. Check that both are on the same Wi-Fi network and that the VPN is off. If the TV does not show up in the list, see Your phone cannot find the TV.

If the TV lost its Wi-Fi

Plug the TV into the router with a network cable and it will use it on its own. Or create a Wi-Fi network with the same name and password as before, because the TV usually reconnects to it by itself.

If the TV is stuck on another input

On your phone, tap Inputs and pick the HDMI. More details in Change the HDMI input on a TV without a remote.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the Magic Remote to use Mando TV?

You only need something to accept the prompt the first time, and the button or joystick on the TV itself works. After that you use just your phone.

Do I need the LG ThinQ app?

No. Mando TV connects directly over your Wi-Fi and does not ask for an LG account.

Can I turn my LG TV off and on from my phone?

Turning it off, yes. To turn it on you need to have turned on "TV On With Mobile" on the TV and to have connected once while the TV was on.
