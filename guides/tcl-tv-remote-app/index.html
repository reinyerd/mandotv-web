TCL TV Remote App for Your Phone

TCL makes the TV, but the software on it is Roku TV or Google TV (Android TV on older sets). Mando TV controls both, so it works as a TCL TV remote app from your phone or PC over your home Wi-Fi. Free, no account, no ads.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Find out which system your TCL runs. If the home screen is a simple grid of channel tiles, it is Roku TV. If it looks like Google TV or Android TV with rows of recommendations, it is Google TV.
  2. Connect your phone and the TCL TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  3. Open Mando TV. The TCL shows up in the list on its own. Tap it.
  4. On a TCL with Roku TV, nothing else is needed. On a TCL with Google TV, type the 6-character code the TV shows on the screen (first time only).
  5. Use the d-pad, OK, Back, Home, volume and keyboard. Turn the TV on from the app if you enabled power-on over the network (see below).

Turn on a TCL TV from your phone

On TCL Roku TV, turn on Fast TV start under Settings, System, Power. On TCL Google TV, look for a Wake on LAN, "Turn on over network" or remote start option in the network or power settings. Some models do not offer it. In that case turn the TV on with the physical power button, usually on the bottom center or on the back.

If your TCL does not show up

Check that your phone and the TV are on the same Wi-Fi network. Routers with separate 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks can hide devices from each other. On an iPhone, allow Local Network for Mando TV in Settings. You can also add the TV by typing its IP address. See your phone cannot find the TV.

Brand pages for your TCL

Setup details are on the Roku remote app page for TCL Roku TV and the Android TV remote app page for TCL Google TV and Android TV.

What is free and what is Pro

The arrows, OK, Back, Home, volume, channels, number keys, keyboard, opening apps, switching inputs and power on or off are free. The touchpad, voice commands, favorite apps, routines and the sleep timer are part of Mando TV Pro, a one-time purchase with no subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Does Mando TV work with every TCL TV?

It works with TCL TVs that run Roku TV or Google TV or Android TV, which is most TCL smart TVs sold in the US. A TCL that is not a smart TV cannot be controlled over Wi-Fi.

Do I need the TCL app or a TCL account?

No. Mando TV connects directly over your home Wi-Fi and needs no account of any kind.

My TCL asks for a code. Where is it?

TCL Google TV and Android TV models show a 6-character code on the TV screen. Type it into Mando TV exactly as it appears. TCL Roku TV does not ask for a code.
