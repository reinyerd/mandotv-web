Roku Remote App Not Working: How to Fix It

If your phone finds the Roku and connects, but no button does anything, it is almost always a setting on the Roku itself that blocks remote apps. Recent Roku versions are more restricted by default. It takes a minute to fix from the Roku menu.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. On the Roku, press Home and go to Settings.
  2. Open System and then Advanced system settings.
  3. Go into "Control by mobile apps". On some models it is called "External control".
  4. Under "Network access", choose Default or Permissive. If on your model it is just a switch, leave it on Enabled.
  5. Go back to Mando TV on your phone and tap your Roku. The buttons now respond.

How to tell if this is your problem

The sign is clear: your phone sees the Roku in the list and connects, but nothing happens when you tap a button. Inside, the Roku answers with error 403, which means "forbidden". Mando TV detects it when it connects and shows you these same steps on the screen.

Default or Permissive, which one do I pick?

Default is enough: it accepts commands that come from your home network, which is what a remote needs. Permissive is more open and is only needed on unusual networks.

If you do not have the Roku remote

When the setting is Disabled, it can only be changed with the physical Roku remote. Borrow one to change it once; after that you can use your phone for everything. See also Lost your Roku remote.

Frequently asked questions

Why did my Roku stop responding if it used to work?

A Roku update can leave that setting more restricted. It is not a problem with your phone or your Wi-Fi: you just need to allow control by apps again.

Does it work for Roku TV and for Roku players?

Yes. The setting is in the same menu on Roku players and on Roku TV sets.

Is it safe to leave it on Default?

Yes. It only accepts commands from devices on your home network.
