Hisense TV Remote App: What Works and What Does Not

Hisense TVs are not all the same inside. Depending on the model, they run Hisense's own VIDAA system, Roku TV, Google TV or Fire TV. Mando TV works as a Hisense TV remote app on all four, with one exception on some recent VIDAA models. Here is how to tell which one you have and what to expect.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Check which system your Hisense runs. VIDAA is Hisense's own interface. Roku TV has a grid of channel tiles. Google TV shows rows of recommendations. Fire TV shows the Fire TV home screen with Amazon rows.
  2. Connect your phone and the Hisense TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  3. Open Mando TV. The TV shows up in the list on its own. Tap it.
  4. VIDAA and Fire TV models may show a 4-digit PIN; Google TV shows a 6-character code. Type it into the app. Roku TV asks for nothing. Do this once.
  5. Use the d-pad, OK, Back, Home, volume and keyboard from your phone.

Hisense with Roku TV or Google TV

These work fully like any other Roku or Google TV. See the Roku remote app page and the Android TV remote app page for the details of each.

Hisense with Fire TV

Fire TV support is new in Mando TV. Update the app to the latest version from your store, and see the Fire TV remote app page for how the PIN pairing works. If something does not behave, write to us from the Help page and tell us your model.

Hisense with VIDAA

Most VIDAA models work: buttons, volume, keyboard, apps and inputs. Some recent VIDAA models only accept Hisense's official app. Mando TV detects that when it connects and tells you plainly, instead of failing silently. In that case the Hisense app for your model, the buttons on the TV or the original remote are the way to go.

Turn on a Hisense from your phone

On Hisense Roku TV, turn on Fast TV start under Settings, System, Power. On Google TV, look for a Wake on LAN or "Turn on over network" option. If neither exists, use the power button on the TV.

What is free and what is Pro

The arrows, OK, Back, Home, volume, channels, number keys, keyboard, opening apps, switching inputs and power on or off are free. The touchpad, voice commands, favorite apps, routines and the sleep timer are part of Mando TV Pro, a one-time purchase with no subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Does Mando TV work with Hisense VIDAA?

Yes, with most VIDAA models. Some recent ones only accept Hisense's official app; Mando TV detects it and tells you. Hisense TVs with Roku TV, Google TV or Fire TV are supported too.

How do I know if my Hisense is Roku TV, Google TV or Fire TV?

Look at the home screen. Roku TV has a plain grid of tiles and a Roku logo at start-up. Google TV has rows of recommendations. Fire TV has the Amazon Fire TV home screen. The model name in Settings, About also tells you.

Do I need a Hisense or Google account?

No. Mando TV connects directly over your home Wi-Fi and has no account of its own.
