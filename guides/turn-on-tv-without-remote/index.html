How to Turn On a TV Without the Remote

Almost every TV still has a physical power button. It is just well hidden. Here is where to look by brand, plus two ways to skip the hunt next time: HDMI-CEC from a console or streaming device, and turning the TV on from your phone over Wi-Fi.

Step by step

  1. Look under the center of the screen, right behind the brand logo. Many TVs have a single small button or joystick there, facing down.
  2. If it is not there, feel along the back of the TV near the lower corners, then along the left and right edges.
  3. Press the button once. On many single-button TVs, once the TV is on, a short press opens a small menu for input and volume, and a long press turns it off.
  4. Still nothing? Check the power cable at both ends and try another wall outlet. If the standby light stays off, the TV is not getting power.
  5. Once the TV is on, set it up so you will not need the button again: turn on HDMI-CEC and power-on over the network, both explained below.

Where the power button is, by brand

Samsung: one control button or stick under the center of the screen, or on the back at a lower corner. LG: a joystick-style button under the LG logo on most models. TCL and Hisense: usually a small button under the middle of the screen or on the back near a lower corner. Sony: on the back or on a side edge, near the bottom. Vizio: on the back, at the lower left or lower right, or on the side. Spots change by model, so if you cannot find it, look up the user manual with the model number printed on the label on the back.

Turn on the TV from a console or streaming device (HDMI-CEC)

With HDMI-CEC on, turning on a PlayStation, an Xbox, a Roku player, a Fire TV Stick or a Chromecast also turns on the TV and switches it to the right input. Each brand gives the setting its own name: Anynet+ on Samsung, SIMPLINK on LG, BRAVIA Sync on Sony, and CEC on most others. More in turn on the TV and switch to your console HDMI.

Turn on the TV from your phone

Smart TVs can wake up over Wi-Fi if you allow it once, and then Mando TV turns them on from your phone. On Samsung, turn on "Power On with Mobile" under Settings, General, Network, Expert Settings. On LG, turn on "TV On With Mobile" (step by step). On a Roku TV, turn on "Fast TV start" under Settings, System, Power. On Google TV and Android TV, look for Wake on LAN or "Turn on over network", if your model has it. You do this once, with the TV on.

If you cannot find any button

Some recent TVs hide the only button so well that it feels like there is none. Look for a small bump or a light sensor area under the screen: the button is often right next to it. Over Wi-Fi, Mando TV needs the TV to have been on and connected once. Infrared is the exception: on an Android phone with an infrared blaster, Mando TV can turn on a TV that was never set up, as long as you point the phone at the TV. Infrared is new, and it works with the brands in the app code list.

Frequently asked questions

Can I turn on a TV with my phone if I never set it up?

Not over Wi-Fi. A TV that is off only wakes up from the network if power-on over the network was turned on beforehand. The first time, use the button on the TV. The exception is an Android phone with an infrared blaster: Mando TV can turn on a TV by infrared with no setup, if the brand is in its code list or you pick "Other brand or I don't know".

How do I turn on a Samsung TV without the remote?

Press the control button under the center of the screen or on the back at a lower corner. Then turn on "Power On with Mobile" so your phone can turn it on next time.

How do I turn on a Roku TV without the remote?

Use the button on the TV, usually under the center or on the back. Then turn on "Fast TV start" under Settings, System, Power so Mando TV can turn it on from your phone.

Why does my TV turn on by itself when I turn on the console?

That is HDMI-CEC. It is handy, and you can turn it off in the TV settings if you prefer.
