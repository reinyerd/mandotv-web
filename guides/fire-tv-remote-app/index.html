Fire TV Remote App for Your Phone or PC

If your Fire TV remote is lost or out of battery, Mando TV can control a Fire TV Stick, a Fire TV Cube or a TV with Fire TV built in from your phone or PC, over your home Wi-Fi. Pairing takes a 4-digit PIN. Fire TV support is new, so update the app first.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Update Mando TV to the latest version from the App Store, Microsoft Store or Google Play.
  2. Turn the Fire TV on and connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi network.
  3. Open Mando TV. Your Fire TV shows up in the list. Tap it.
  4. The TV screen shows a 4-digit PIN. Type it into Mando TV. You only do this once.
  5. Use the d-pad, OK, Back, Home, playback buttons and the keyboard to type searches.

Fire TV devices it is meant for

Fire TV Stick, Fire TV Stick 4K, Fire TV Cube and TVs with Fire TV built in, such as Amazon Fire TV Edition sets and some Hisense models. Support is new, and we have limited real-device testing. If something does not behave, tell us your model from the Help page.

Turning on a Fire TV

Mando TV does not turn a Fire TV on over the network. Turn on the TV with its power button: a Fire TV Stick or Cube plugged into it usually wakes up with it through HDMI-CEC, and on a TV with Fire TV built in, the TV is the Fire TV. Then control it from your phone.

If your Fire TV does not show up

Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network and that the Fire TV is awake. On an iPhone, allow Local Network for Mando TV in Settings. See your phone cannot find the TV.

No remote at all?

You need the Fire TV screen to show the PIN, so the TV and the Fire TV must be on and showing the picture. If the TV is on a wrong input, change it with the TV buttons. See change the HDMI input without a remote.

What is free and what is Pro

The arrows, OK, Back, Home, volume, channels, number keys, keyboard, opening apps, switching inputs and power on or off are free. The touchpad, voice commands, favorite apps, routines and the sleep timer are part of Mando TV Pro, a one-time purchase with no subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the Amazon Fire TV app?

No. Mando TV connects to the Fire TV over your Wi-Fi with a PIN. You do not need an Amazon account on the phone.

Does it work with Fire TV Edition TVs and Fire TV sticks?

Yes, that is what it was built for. Support is new, so keep the app updated and tell us about any model that does not behave.

Why do I need a PIN?

The Fire TV shows a 4-digit PIN to prove you are in front of the TV. Nobody can pair without seeing the screen.
