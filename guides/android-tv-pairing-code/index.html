Android TV Asks for a Pairing Code: What to Do

The first time you connect your phone to an Android TV or Google TV, the TV shows a 6-character code on the screen. This is normal and happens only once: it makes sure nobody else can control your TV. Here is how to enter it and what to do if it does not show up.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Turn on the TV and leave the home screen in view.
  2. Open Mando TV on your phone, on the same Wi-Fi as the TV, and tap your TV in the list.
  3. Look at the TV screen: a code of 6 letters and numbers appears.
  4. Type it on your phone exactly as shown and tap Pair. The letters only go from A to F, so a round character is always the number zero.
  5. Done. The TV remembers your phone and does not ask for the code again.

If the code does not show up on the TV

Check that the TV is really on and not in standby, and that your phone is on the same Wi-Fi. Tap the TV on your phone again. If it still does not appear, restart the TV: unplug it for ten seconds, plug it back in and try again.

If it says the code is wrong

The code changes every time it is requested. If you took a while or requested it twice, only the latest one on the screen works. Tap the TV on your phone again and type the new code.

If the TV asks for the code again

This happens when the TV is factory reset or when you clear the app data on your phone. Pair again and it is saved. If you have no remote at all, see Android TV without a remote.

Frequently asked questions

Which TVs use this code?

The ones with Android TV or Google TV: Sony, Philips, TCL, Hisense, Sharp, Xiaomi, Chromecast with Google TV and several more.

Do I need a Google account on my phone?

No. The code on the screen is all you need.

Is it safe?

Yes. The code proves you are in front of the TV: nobody can pair without seeing it on the screen.
