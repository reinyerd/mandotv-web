SmartThings TV Remote Alternative for Samsung TVs

SmartThings is Samsung's app for the whole smart home, and its TV remote works through your Samsung account. If that remote stopped working, or you just want a quick one, Mando TV controls Samsung TVs with Tizen, 2016 models and newer, directly over your home Wi-Fi with no account.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Connect your phone and the Samsung TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Download Mando TV and open it. Your Samsung TV appears in the list on its own. Tap it.
  3. The TV asks whether to allow the connection. Choose Allow with the remote or with the control button on the TV (first time only).
  4. Use the d-pad, OK, Return, Home, volume, channels and the keyboard right away.
  5. Optional: turn on "Power On with Mobile" under Settings, General, Network, Expert Settings so Mando TV can also turn the TV on.

Why a phone remote stops working

The usual causes are simple: the phone and the TV are on different Wi-Fi networks, a VPN is on, or the TV is in a deep standby mode. With SmartThings, the Samsung account adds one more thing that can fail, such as being signed out. Mando TV talks to the TV directly inside your home network, so there is no account to sign in to. More fixes in your phone cannot find the TV.

Mando TV vs SmartThings, in plain terms

Keep SmartThings if you use it for Samsung appliances, lights or other smart home devices, or for Samsung account features. Use Mando TV when you just want a fast remote for the TV, or when you also have an LG, Roku, Google TV or Fire TV at home: one app controls all of them. Both can live on the same phone.

If you denied the permission by mistake

On the TV, go to Settings, General, External Device Manager, Device Connection Manager, and set Mando TV to allowed.

No remote at all?

You can accept the prompt with the control button on the TV itself. The guide Samsung TV without a remote walks through it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I control my Samsung TV without SmartThings?

Yes. Mando TV connects to Samsung Tizen TVs directly over your Wi-Fi. You allow the connection once on the TV, with no Samsung account.

Does Mando TV control Samsung appliances?

No. It is a TV remote only. Keep SmartThings for appliances and other smart home devices.

Is Mando TV made by Samsung?

No. Mando TV is an independent app and is not affiliated with Samsung. Samsung and SmartThings belong to their owners and are named only to describe compatibility.
