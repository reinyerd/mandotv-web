Your Phone Cannot Find the TV: What to Check

You open the remote app and the list is empty, or the TV shows up but the connection drops. It is almost always the network, not the TV. Check these points in order: the first one solves most cases.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Look at which Wi-Fi your phone is on and which one the TV is on. It has to be the same network, with the same name. A guest network will not work, because it keeps devices apart.
  2. Turn off the VPN on your phone, if you use one. With the VPN on, your phone goes out through another network and cannot see the devices in your home, or the connection drops after a few seconds.
  3. Turn off mobile data for a moment. Some phones switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data on their own when the signal is weak.
  4. Turn on the TV and wait until it finishes starting up. A TV that is off does not answer the search.
  5. On an iPhone, check that the app has Local Network permission: Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.
  6. If it still does not show up, add it by hand: in Mando TV tap "Add by IP" and type the IP address of the TV.

Let the app walk you through it

In the TV list, tap the "TV not showing up?" button. The app checks the most common causes step by step (same Wi-Fi, VPN, local network permission and more) and gives you a "Check again" button after each fix. It is free.

Where to find the IP address of the TV

It is in the TV network menu and changes a little by model. On LG: Settings, Network, Wi-Fi Connection, Advanced Wi-Fi Settings. On Samsung: Settings, General, Network, Network Status, IP Settings. On Roku: Settings, Network, About. On Android TV and Google TV: Settings, Network & Internet, then tap your network. It is a number like 192.168.1.86.

If it shows up but the connection drops

When the connection drops after a few seconds, the cause is usually on the phone: a VPN, a switch to another Wi-Fi network or battery saver. Turn off the VPN, stay on the same Wi-Fi and try again.

2.4 and 5 GHz Wi-Fi

It does not matter if your phone is on the 5 GHz network and the TV is on the 2.4 GHz one, as long as both come from the same router. If you have a range extender and the TV still does not show up, move your phone closer to the main router and try again.

Frequently asked questions

Can I control the TV with mobile data?

No. The remote signal travels over your home Wi-Fi, so your phone and the TV must be on the same network.

Why does it not work with the VPN on?

Because the VPN sends all of your phone traffic through another network and the phone can no longer see the devices in your home. Turn it off while you use the remote.

Does Bluetooth or infrared work?

Wi-Fi is how Mando TV controls smart TVs. Infrared works only on Android phones that have an infrared blaster, and it is meant for TVs that are not smart TVs. Bluetooth is available in beta, for free and only on Android 9 or newer: the phone connects to the TV as a Bluetooth keyboard and remote, with no Wi-Fi needed. It works with Google TV, Android TV and Fire TV; other brands that accept Bluetooth keyboards may work, but Roku does not accept them.
