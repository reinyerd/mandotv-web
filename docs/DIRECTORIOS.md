- **Nombre:** Mando TV

- **Correo de contacto o verificación:** hola@mandotv.app (está en el dominio, sirve para "verificar el producto")
- **Eslogan (49 caracteres):** One universal TV remote on your phone. No ads.
- **Descripción corta (160):** Universal TV remote for Samsung, LG, Roku, Google TV, Fire TV and Hisense, on your phone or computer over Wi-Fi. No ads, no account.
- **Descripción larga:**

```
Mando TV is a universal TV remote app for phone and computer. It controls Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS), Roku, Google TV / Android TV, Fire TV and Hisense TVs over your home Wi-Fi, so one app replaces the remote app of each brand.

There is no account and no ads. The app talks directly to the TV on your local network.

Free: remote buttons, phone keyboard, apps, inputs and turning the TV on over the network, plus an easy mode with big buttons.
Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription): touchpad, voice, routines, sleep timer, night mode, photos and videos to the TV and more.

Available on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows. Android is in early access on Google Play.
Mando TV is an independent app, not affiliated with any TV maker.
```
