Mando TV / Android TV and Google TV

Android TV Remote App for Your Phone

Mando TV is an Android TV remote app: it turns your phone or computer into the remote for your Android TV or Google TV, over your home Wi-Fi. It also works from an iPhone and from Windows.

Compatible TVs

TVs with Google TV or Android TV from Sony, Philips, TCL, Hisense, Sharp, Xiaomi, Toshiba, Panasonic, Haier, Skyworth, JVC, Thomson, Motorola, OnePlus, iFFALCON, Kogan, Vu and other brands, plus Chromecast with Google TV, Google TV Streamer, onn. and Nvidia Shield. Some of these brands also sell models with a different system: if your TV shows the Google TV home screen or has the Google Play store, Mando TV can control it.

How to connect your Android TV and Google TV to Mando TV

  1. Connect your phone and the TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open Mando TV: your Android TV shows up in the list on its own. Tap it.
  3. The TV shows a 6-character code. Type it into Mando TV (first time only).

Turn on your Android TV and Google TV from your phone

It depends on the manufacturer. Look for "Wake on LAN", "Turn on over network" or "Remote start" in the network or power settings. Some models do not have it. After that, when you tap the TV in Mando TV while it is off, the app asks if you want to turn it on and connects by itself.

Android TV and Google TV questions

Does it work with Chromecast with Google TV?

Yes, it pairs with the 6-character code that appears on the screen.

How is it different from the Google TV app?

Mando TV does not need a Google account, it also works from Windows, and it controls LG, Samsung and Roku TVs from the same app.

The code does not show up on the screen. What do I do?

Close Mando TV, open it again and tap the TV once more. Check that the TV is not in a deep power-saving mode.
