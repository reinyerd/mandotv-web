Mando TV / Roku

Roku Remote App for Your Phone

Mando TV is a Roku remote app: it turns your phone or computer into the remote for your Roku, over your home Wi-Fi. It works with Roku TV sets and with Roku players that plug into HDMI.

Compatible TVs

Roku TV sets (TCL, Hisense, Philips, Sharp, JVC and others) and Roku players (Express, Streaming Stick, Ultra).

What you can do with your Roku

  • All the buttonsD-pad, OK, Back, Home, volume, mute, replay and playback.
  • KeyboardType searches in Roku channels with your phone keyboard.
  • Channels (Pro)Open Netflix, YouTube and your other Roku channels with one tap.
  • Inputs (Pro)On a Roku TV, switch to HDMI, antenna or AV from the app.
  • Turn on the TVOn Roku TV sets with fast start, Mando TV turns them on from your phone.
  • No pairingRoku does not ask for a code: tap it in the list and you are in.

How to connect your Roku to Mando TV

  1. Connect your phone and the Roku to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open Mando TV: your Roku shows up in the list on its own. Tap it.
  3. If it does not connect, on the Roku go to Settings, System, Advanced system settings, Control by mobile apps, and set network access to Default or Permissive.

Turn on your Roku from your phone

On Roku TV sets, turn on "Fast TV start" under Settings, System, Power. Roku players connected to another TV cannot be turned on this way. After that, when you tap the TV in Mando TV while it is off, the app asks if you want to turn it on and connects by itself.

Roku questions

Does it work with the Roku Express or the Streaming Stick?

Yes. All Roku players and Roku TV sets are controlled the same way.

Mando TV cannot find my Roku. What do I do?

Check that both are on the same Wi-Fi network and that "Control by mobile apps" on the Roku is not turned off. You can also add it by typing its IP address.

Do I need a Roku account?

No. Mando TV connects directly over your Wi-Fi.
