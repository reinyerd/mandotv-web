Privacy

In short: Mando TV does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no ads, no trackers and no servers of ours that store what you do.

Effective November 14, 2026. Last updated: November 21, 2026.

Who we are

Mando TV is an app to control your TV from your phone or computer. This policy explains what information the app uses and what it does with it. If you have questions, write to us at hola@mandotv.app.

What data we collect

The app: none. The app does not send information about you or about how you use it to us or to third parties. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

This website: we measure visits in two ways, explained in the section Cookies on this website.

What the app stores on your device

To work, the app stores the following only on your phone or computer:

  • The TVs you added: name, brand, IP address inside your network and network hardware address (MAC), which is used to turn it on.
  • The connection keys that each TV hands over when you accept the permission. They are stored encrypted in the secure storage of the system (Keychain on iPhone and Mac, Keystore on Android, protected storage on Windows).
  • Your preferences: light or dark theme, background color, vibration and type of remote.
  • If you use the Pro features: your favorite apps, your routines and your latest YouTube searches (you can delete them from the app).

If you delete the app, all of this is deleted with it.

Who the app talks to

With your TVs, inside your home Wi-Fi network. To find them, the app searches your local network; that is why on an iPhone it asks for the "Local Network" permission. The text you type with the app keyboard goes straight to the TV and is not stored.

In addition, when it opens, the app downloads a small public file from this website (mandotv.app/app/config.json) to find out whether there is a new version you should install and which features belong to Mando TV Pro. It is a download, like opening a page: the app does not send your data, your TVs or what you do. It only states the app version and the system (for example, Android), so it can show you the right notice. If you have no internet, the app keeps working with the last file it downloaded; if more than 30 days pass without being able to download it, it will ask you to connect to the internet for a moment.

If you send photos or videos to the TV (a Pro feature), the TV downloads only the files you pick, straight from your phone or PC over your home Wi-Fi. They do not go through the internet or through us, the app does not see the rest of your photos, and sharing stops when you close that screen.

Microphone and voice

Voice search uses the microphone only while you tap the speak button. What you say is converted to text by the speech recognition of your system: Apple on iPhone and Mac and Google on Android, under their own privacy policies. On Windows, the voice is recognized on your own PC and never leaves it; only on very old processors does the app use Windows voice typing from Microsoft, under its privacy policy. Mando TV does not record or store the audio, and the resulting text goes straight to your TV, just like what you type with the keyboard.

Test report

The app has a "Copy test report" button to help us fix errors. The report is kept only in the memory of the app while it is open and is never sent on its own: you decide whether to copy it and share it. It does not include connection keys, pairing codes or anything you type; only the steps the app took, such as searching, connecting and the errors.

Cookies on this website

This applies only to the mandotv.app website, not to the app.

No cookies, always

We count visits anonymously with Cloudflare Web Analytics: how many people visit, which pages they read and from which country. It does not use cookies, it does not use your IP address to identify you and it does not follow what you do on other sites.

With cookies, only if you accept

If you tap "Accept" on the cookie notice, we turn on Google Analytics (from Google LLC) to better understand how the website is used: page views, time on the page, device type, approximate city and where you came from. To do this it stores the _ga and _ga_* cookies in your browser for up to 2 years. Google may process this data on servers outside Chile. We turned off the advertising features and Google Signals, and we do not use this data to show you ads.

If you tap "Decline" or choose nothing, Google Analytics is not loaded and no cookie is stored. You can change your decision at any time with the "Cookies" button at the bottom of every page; if you withdraw permission, we delete those cookies.

Your choice is stored in your browser (local storage, not sent to anyone) so that we do not ask you again.

Purchases

If you buy Mando TV Pro, the payment is processed by the app store (App Store, Google Play or Microsoft Store). We do not receive or store your card details. The store only confirms to the app that the purchase exists.

Emails you send us

If you write to us at hola@mandotv.app, we use your email address and what you tell us only to reply to you. We do not share it or use it for advertising. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If one day what the app does with your data changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.
