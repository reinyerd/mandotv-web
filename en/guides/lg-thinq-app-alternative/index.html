LG ThinQ App Alternative for Your LG TV Remote

LG ThinQ is LG's official app, built to run a whole smart home: appliances, the TV and more. If all you want is a clean remote for your LG TV, Mando TV does that one job over your home Wi-Fi, with no LG account to create and no ads.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Connect your phone (iPhone, Android or Windows PC) and your LG TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Download Mando TV and open it. Your LG TV shows up in the list on its own. Tap it.
  3. A permission prompt appears on the TV screen. Accept it once with the TV remote, the joystick under the screen or the Magic Remote.
  4. Use the buttons, the volume and the keyboard right away. No LG account and no TV registration are needed.
  5. Optional: turn on "TV On With Mobile" in the TV settings so you can also switch the TV on from your phone.

Mando TV vs LG ThinQ, in plain terms

Use ThinQ if you want to manage LG appliances such as a washer, a fridge or an air conditioner, or LG account features. Use Mando TV if you want a fast, simple remote for the TV that also works for Samsung, Roku and Google TV in the same app. Many people keep both: ThinQ for appliances, Mando TV for the TV.

What Mando TV does on an LG TV

It works with LG smart TVs running webOS, which covers most LG smart TV models since 2014, including LED, NanoCell, QNED and OLED. It gives you the d-pad, OK, Back, Home, volume, channels, mute, playback controls and a keyboard for typing in YouTube, Netflix or the browser. The apps list and the inputs list are free too. With Pro you also get a pointer like the Magic Remote.

What Mando TV does not do

It does not control LG appliances, it does not use your LG account and it does not replace the LG account services on the TV. It is a remote, not a smart home hub.

Privacy and ads

Mando TV has no ads and no account. It talks to the TV inside your home network and keeps the TV connection key encrypted on your own device. The LG TV remote page has the full list of features.

What is free and what is Pro

The arrows, OK, Back, Home, volume, channels, number keys, keyboard, opening apps, switching inputs and power on or off are free. The touchpad, voice commands, favorite apps, routines and the sleep timer are part of Mando TV Pro, a one-time purchase with no subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Can I control my LG TV without the LG ThinQ app?

Yes. Mando TV connects to LG webOS TVs directly over your Wi-Fi. You accept one prompt on the TV the first time and that is it, with no LG account.

Does Mando TV work with the LG Magic Remote pointer?

Mando TV Pro has a pointer you move with your finger on the phone screen, similar to how the Magic Remote moves the arrow.

Is Mando TV made by LG?

No. Mando TV is an independent app and is not affiliated with LG. LG and ThinQ belong to their owners and are named only to describe compatibility.
