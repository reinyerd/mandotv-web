How to Control Your TV From a Windows PC

If you spend the day in front of your computer, you do not need to look for the remote: Mando TV for Windows controls your Samsung, LG, Roku, Google TV or Android TV over your home Wi-Fi. It is handy for switching apps, turning up the volume or typing in YouTube with your PC keyboard.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Download Mando TV free from Microsoft Store on your Windows 10 or 11 computer.
  2. Check that the computer and the TV are on the same network (Wi-Fi or cable, but through the same router).
  3. Open Mando TV: your TV shows up in the list on its own. Tap or click it.
  4. Accept the connection on the TV screen the first time (on Google TV and Android TV, type the code the TV shows).
  5. Done: use the buttons or the keyboard to type in the TV search box. The touchpad is a Pro feature.

PC keyboard shortcuts (Pro)

With Mando TV Pro you control the TV with your computer keyboard: arrow keys to move around, Enter for OK, Backspace or Esc to go back, + and - for the volume, M to mute and Space to pause.

Mini remote always on top (Pro)

Keep a small window with the remote always visible while you work or play on your PC.

Talk to your TV from the PC (Pro)

With Mando TV Pro you give the TV commands with your PC microphone: "open YouTube", "turn it up" or "turn off in 30 minutes", said your own way. On Windows the voice is recognized on the PC itself: it needs no internet, no Windows settings to change, and it works in the 6 languages of the app. What you say never leaves the PC and is not stored. Only on very old processors does the app fall back to Windows voice typing, which needs "Online speech recognition" turned on in Settings > Privacy & security > Speech and the language added in Windows. In that case, if something is missing, the app tells you and opens the setting with one button.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a cable or Bluetooth to control the TV from my PC?

No. Mando TV connects over your home network: the PC and the TV only need to be on the same router.

Does it work with any TV?

It works with Samsung TVs running Tizen (2016 and newer), LG with webOS, Roku, and Google TV or Android TV (Sony, Philips, TCL, Hisense, Sharp, Xiaomi, Toshiba, Panasonic, Haier, Skyworth and others).

How much does it cost?

The buttons, the keyboard and power-on over the network are free and ad-free. Mando TV Pro is a one-time purchase, no subscription, for extras such as the touchpad, the PC keyboard shortcuts and the mini remote.

Why does the microphone not understand me on Windows?

Check that voice is on (it is part of Mando TV Pro) and that the PC microphone works in Windows. On most PCs the voice is recognized on the PC itself, with no internet. Only on very old processors does the app use Windows voice typing: then check that "Online speech recognition" is on in Settings > Privacy & security > Speech and that Windows has a language that supports voice typing (Settings > Time & language > Language & region). Mando TV switches to it only while it listens.
