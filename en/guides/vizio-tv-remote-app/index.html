Vizio TV Remote App: Honest Answer and Real Options

Mando TV does not control Vizio SmartCast TVs directly, and we would rather say that plainly than waste your time. Here is what does work for a Vizio TV with a lost remote, and how Mando TV can still help if you use a streaming stick.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Turn the TV on with its physical power button. On most Vizio TVs it is on the back or under the screen, on the lower edge or a corner.
  2. Install the official Vizio phone app for your TV model and follow its setup. It is the supported way to control SmartCast over Wi-Fi.
  3. If the app cannot find the TV, check that your phone and the TV are on the same Wi-Fi network, then restart the router and the TV.
  4. If you plug a Roku or Google TV streaming device into the Vizio, install Mando TV and control that device instead, over the same Wi-Fi.
  5. To switch the TV to the right HDMI input, use the input button on the TV itself, then control the streaming device from your phone.

Why Mando TV does not list Vizio

Mando TV controls TVs and devices that run Roku, Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, Google TV or Android TV. Vizio SmartCast uses a different system that Mando TV does not support, so we do not claim it.

How Mando TV can still help with a Vizio

Many Vizio TVs have a Roku stick or a Chromecast with Google TV plugged into an HDMI port. Mando TV controls the Roku and Google TV devices. That covers the streaming apps. For TV power and HDMI inputs on the Vizio itself, you still need the Vizio app or the physical buttons.

Other ways to control a Vizio without the remote

Use the buttons on the TV. Check whether your phone has an infrared blaster. Some Android phones do, with an app that supports Vizio codes. Or order a replacement remote that matches your model number, which is on the label on the back of the TV.

Frequently asked questions

Does Mando TV work with Vizio TVs?

No. Mando TV does not control Vizio SmartCast TVs. It controls Roku and Google TV devices plugged into them.

Will Vizio support be added?

We do not announce support before it works. This page will say so plainly when it changes.

What is the fastest way to control a Vizio TV with no remote?

Use the physical power and input buttons on the TV, and the official Vizio phone app for everything else.
