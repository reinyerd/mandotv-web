Roku Mobile App Alternative: One Remote for Every TV

The official Roku mobile app does a lot: channel store, casting, private listening and a remote. If you only want a fast remote, or you also have a Samsung, LG or Google TV in the house, Mando TV is a simple alternative that controls Roku over your Wi-Fi with no account.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Connect your phone and the Roku to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Download Mando TV and open it. Your Roku TV or Roku player shows up in the list. Tap it. Roku does not ask for a code.
  3. Use the d-pad, OK, Back, Home, volume and the keyboard. Type searches in Roku channels with your phone keyboard.
  4. If the buttons do nothing, on the Roku go to Settings, System, Advanced system settings, Control by mobile apps, and set network access to Default or Permissive (on newer Roku software, set Control by mobile apps to Enabled).
  5. Optional: on a Roku TV, turn on Fast TV start under Settings, System, Power so Mando TV can switch the TV on.

Where Mando TV fits better

One app for several TVs: Roku, Samsung, LG and Google TV or Android TV. No account, no ads. It also works from a Windows PC, which is handy at a desk. The d-pad and volume feel quick, and the keyboard makes typing a search painless.

Where the official Roku app is better

Keep the Roku app for what it offers beyond the remote, such as browsing and adding channels from the Roku Channel Store and its own casting and private listening features. Use both if you like. They can run on the same phone.

Roku TV sets and Roku players

Both work. That includes Roku TV models from TCL, Hisense, Philips and others, and Roku Express, Streaming Stick and Ultra. Details are on the Roku remote app page.

If the Roku app stopped working

If the buttons stopped responding, the Roku is usually blocking control by apps. The guide Roku remote app not working walks through the setting that fixes it.

What is free and what is Pro

The arrows, OK, Back, Home, volume, channels, number keys, keyboard, opening apps, switching inputs and power on or off are free. The touchpad, voice commands, favorite apps, routines and the sleep timer are part of Mando TV Pro, a one-time purchase with no subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Roku account to use Mando TV?

No. Mando TV talks to the Roku over your home network and does not ask for an account.

Does Mando TV replace the Roku mobile app completely?

For the remote, yes. For the channel store, casting and private listening, keep the official Roku app.

Will it work on my Roku Express or Streaming Stick?

Yes. All Roku players and Roku TV sets are controlled the same way.
