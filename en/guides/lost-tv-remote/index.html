Lost Your TV Remote? What You Can Do

A lost TV remote happens in every home: it disappears just when you want to watch something. The good news is that your phone can work as the remote, over your home Wi-Fi. Here is what to do, in order.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Turn on the TV with its physical button. It is almost always at the bottom center, or on the back near a corner. Sometimes it is a single button and sometimes a tiny joystick.
  2. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi network as the TV.
  3. Download Mando TV, which is free and does not ask for an account, and open it. Your TV shows up in the list on its own. Tap it.
  4. Accept the connection for your brand. On LG and Samsung the TV shows a prompt: accept it with the button or joystick on the TV itself. On Android TV and Google TV you type a 6-character code that appears on the screen. Roku does not ask for anything.
  5. Done. The arrows, OK, Back, Home, volume, channels and keyboard are free.
  6. For next time, turn on power-on over the network in the TV settings (your brand guide is below) and you can turn the TV on from your phone without touching it.

If the TV does not turn on with the button

Before you look for another remote, check that the cable is plugged in firmly and that the wall outlet has power. If the button on the TV does nothing, the problem is the TV and not the remote.

If the remote just stopped working

Try new batteries. To check whether it still sends a signal, point it at your phone camera and press a button: on many phones you will see a purple light on the screen. If the light shows up and the TV does not respond, the sensor on the TV may be blocked.

If you changed routers and the TV lost its Wi-Fi

Without a remote it is harder to connect the TV to the new network. The easiest way is to plug the TV into the router with a network cable. You can also create a Wi-Fi network with the same name and password as before: the TV usually reconnects to it by itself.

Frequently asked questions

Can I turn on the TV with my phone if I never connected it before?

This first time, turn it on with the button on the TV. After you connect your phone and turn on power-on over the network in the TV settings, you can turn it on from the app.

Do I need to buy a new remote?

Not necessarily. With Mando TV you get buttons, volume, keyboard and power-on for free. If you still prefer a physical one, look for the exact model of your TV.

Does it work if the TV has no Wi-Fi?

No. The phone and the TV have to be on the same Wi-Fi network. A TV that is not a smart TV cannot be controlled with Mando TV.
