Android TV Without a Remote: How to Use Your Phone

If you lost your Android TV or Google TV remote, or it ran out of batteries, your phone replaces it. The best part is that connecting does not need the remote: the TV shows a code on the screen and you type it on your phone. It works on Sony, Philips, TCL, Hisense, Sharp and Xiaomi TVs with Android TV, Chromecast with Google TV and Nvidia Shield.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Turn on the TV with its physical button. It is almost always at the bottom center, or on the back near a corner.
  2. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi network as the TV.
  3. Download Mando TV, which is free and does not ask for a Google account, and open it. Tap your TV in the list.
  4. The TV shows a code of 6 letters and numbers. Type it on your phone and tap Pair. You do not need the remote for this.
  5. Now you can use the arrows, OK, Back, Home, volume, mute and keyboard. They are free, and so is the apps list. The touchpad is Pro.
  6. To turn it on from your phone next time, look for "Wake on LAN", "Turn on over network" or "Remote start" in the TV network or power settings.

If your TV does not turn on from your phone

Not every Android TV has power-on over the network; it depends on the manufacturer and the model. If yours does not have it, turn it on with the button on the TV and continue from your phone. You can turn it off from the app.

Chromecast with Google TV, Google TV Streamer and Shield

They connect the same way: same Wi-Fi network, tap the device in the list and type the code. If the TV shows another input, switch it to the HDMI where the device is plugged in, as explained in Change the HDMI input on a TV without a remote.

If the code does not show up or it says it is wrong

Check that the TV is really on and not in standby. The code changes every time it is requested: only the latest one works. There are more details in Android TV asks for a pairing code.

Google app or Mando TV

Google has its own app that needs a Google account. Mando TV does not ask for an account, it also works from Windows, and it controls LG, Samsung and Roku TVs from the same app.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the remote to pair my phone?

No. The code appears on the TV screen and you type it on your phone. You only have to turn on the TV, with the button on the TV if needed.

Does it work with a Hisense?

On Hisense TVs that have Android TV or Google TV, yes. Hisense TVs with another system are not compatible.

Can I type with my phone keyboard?

Yes. The keyboard is free and works for searching in YouTube, Netflix and the rest of the apps.
