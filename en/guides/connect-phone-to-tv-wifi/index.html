How to Connect Your Phone to Your TV Over Wi-Fi as a Remote

All you need is your phone and your TV on the same Wi-Fi network, plus a remote app. Mando TV finds your TV on its own and works with Samsung, LG, Roku, Google TV and Android TV.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Connect your phone and the TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open Mando TV. Your TV shows up in the list on its own. Tap it.
  3. Accept the connection for your brand (the details for each one are below).
  4. Use the buttons or your phone keyboard to control the TV. The touchpad is a Pro feature.

Samsung

The TV asks if you allow the connection. Accept it on the screen the first time. It works with Tizen, 2016 and newer. To turn it on from your phone, turn on "Power On with Mobile" under Settings, General, Network, Expert Settings.

LG

The TV shows a prompt asking for permission. Accept it the first time. To turn it on from your phone, look for "TV On With Mobile" in Settings and turn on "Turn on via Wi-Fi".

Roku

Roku does not ask you to pair: tap it in the list and you are in. To turn it on, turn on "Fast TV start" under Settings, System, Power.

Google TV and Android TV

The TV shows a code on the screen. Type it into Mando TV the first time. To turn it on, look for "Wake on LAN", "Turn on over network" or "Network wake-up" in the settings.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the internet to use my phone as a remote?

You need your phone and the TV on the same Wi-Fi network. Mando TV connects straight to your TV over that network.

Why does my TV not show up in the list?

Check that the TV is on and that your phone is not using mobile data or a different Wi-Fi network. Some routers have a guest network that keeps devices apart.

Can I use Mando TV from an iPhone or an Android phone?

Yes on iPhone and iPad: it is already on the App Store. It is also on Microsoft Store for Windows. Android is coming soon: you can join the free early access on Google Play.
