Samsung TV Without the Original Remote: Use Your Phone

If you lost your Samsung TV remote or it stopped working, you can use your phone as the remote over Wi-Fi. It works on Samsung TVs running Tizen, 2016 models and newer.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Turn on the TV with its physical button. On most models it is at the bottom or on the back, sometimes as a small joystick.
  2. Check that the TV is connected to your Wi-Fi and that your phone is on the same network.
  3. Open Mando TV on your phone. Your Samsung TV shows up in the list on its own. Tap it.
  4. The TV asks you to accept the connection. On most models you accept it with the physical buttons on the TV or with the joystick on the back.
  5. Done, you can now use your phone as the remote. The buttons, the keyboard and the apps are free; the touchpad is a Pro feature.
  6. To turn it on from your phone next time, go to Settings, General, Network, Expert Settings and turn on "Power On with Mobile".

If the TV does not ask for permission

The permission may already be saved, or the TV may be on a different Wi-Fi network. Close Mando TV and open it again with the TV on. If the TV is older than 2016, it uses a different system that is not supported yet.

If you denied the permission by mistake

On the TV go to Settings, General, External Device Manager, Device Connection Manager, and set Mando TV to allowed. If your phone still cannot find the TV, see Your phone cannot find the TV.

Frequently asked questions

How do I accept the connection if I have no remote?

On most models you accept it with the physical buttons on the TV or with the joystick on the back. If your model has no buttons, write to hola@mandotv.app and we will help you.

Does Mando TV ask for a Samsung account?

No. It connects directly over your Wi-Fi, with no Samsung account.

Does it work with my older Samsung TV?

It works with Samsung TVs running Tizen, 2016 and newer.
