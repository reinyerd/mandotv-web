How to Turn On an LG TV With Your Phone

You can turn on an LG TV with your phone, but first you have to switch on one setting on the TV. Once that is done, your phone turns on the LG TV over your Wi-Fi network, with no remote. It takes five minutes and you only do it once.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Connect your phone and the LG TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. On the TV, open Settings and look for "TV On With Mobile". It is under Settings, General, Devices, External Devices (on older models Connection, Mobile Connection Management).
  3. Switch on "Turn on via Wi-Fi". If your TV is connected with a network cable, switch on the wired option instead.
  4. If your TV has "Quick Start+", turn it on too.
  5. With the TV on, open Mando TV on your phone and tap your LG TV in the list. Accept the prompt that appears on the screen. That is how the app learns to wake it up.
  6. Turn the TV off and test it: tap the TV in Mando TV and the app asks if you want to turn it on.

If it does not turn on

The TV must have been on the same Wi-Fi network when it was turned off. Check that the setting is still on and that your phone is on that same network. If you changed routers, connect once more with the TV on.

Frequently asked questions

Can I turn on the LG TV with my phone if I never connected it?

No. First you have to turn on "TV On With Mobile" on the TV and connect once with Mando TV while the TV is on.

Do I need the LG ThinQ app?

No. Mando TV connects directly over your Wi-Fi and does not ask for an LG account.

Does it work with mobile data?

No. The phone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network.
