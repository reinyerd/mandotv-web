Guides to Use Your Phone as a TV Remote

Simple steps to connect, turn on and fix the remote of your Samsung, LG, Roku or Android TV with Mando TV.

All guides

  • How to turn on an LG TV with your phone: Turn on your LG TV with your phone over Wi-Fi: enable "TV On With Mobile" on the TV and use Mando TV. Step by step, free, no ads.
  • Lost your TV remote: what you can do: Lost your TV remote? Turn the TV on with its button and use your phone as the remote. Steps for LG, Samsung, Roku and Android TV. Free, no ads.
  • Lost your LG TV remote: how to use your phone: No LG TV remote? Turn the TV on with its button, accept the prompt and use your phone as the remote over Wi-Fi. No LG account, no ads.
  • Change the HDMI input on a TV without a remote: Change the HDMI input without a remote on Samsung, LG or Roku: use the button on the TV or your phone. Free to start, no ads.
  • Android TV without a remote: how to use your phone: No Android TV or Google TV remote? Connect your phone with the 6-character code on the screen. Free, no account, no ads.
  • How to turn on the TV and switch to your console HDMI without the remote: Launch day and no TV remote? Turn on your TV and switch to your PS5 or Xbox HDMI input from your phone. Free, no ads.
