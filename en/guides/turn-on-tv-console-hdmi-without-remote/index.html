How to turn on the TV and switch to your console's HDMI without the remote

GTA VI launch day, the console ready to go, and the TV remote lost somewhere in the couch. It happens more than you would think. With Mando TV your phone turns on the TV and switches it to your PlayStation or Xbox input, over your home Wi-Fi.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi network as the TV.
  2. Open Mando TV and tap your TV in the list (Samsung, LG, Roku or Android TV).
  3. If the TV is off, tap Turn on. This works if the TV has power-on over the network enabled. If you never turned that on, switch the TV on this one time with the button on the TV itself, and enable it for next time.
  4. Turn on the console. If HDMI-CEC is enabled (see the free trick below), the TV switches to its input on its own.
  5. If it does not switch, tap Inputs in Mando TV and pick the HDMI port the console is plugged into: HDMI 1, HDMI 2 and so on.
  6. For the volume, use the big buttons in the app while you play.

The free trick: let the console switch the TV for you

The PS5 and the Xbox can turn on the TV and jump to their HDMI input as soon as they start, thanks to HDMI-CEC. On the PS5 it is under Settings > System > HDMI > Enable HDMI Device Link. On the Xbox, open Settings > General > TV & display options and look for the device control option. On the TV the feature has a different name depending on the brand: Anynet+ on Samsung, SIMPLINK on LG, and BRAVIA Sync or HDMI control on others.

If the TV stays on another screen

If the console is on but the TV still shows the antenna or an app, the TV did not change input. From your phone, tap Inputs and choose the console HDMI. If you prefer, use the arrows and OK to reach the TV inputs, or press the button on the TV itself, and pick the HDMI. The number is the port where you plugged in the console cable.

If it is a Roku TV

The physical button on the TV, usually at the bottom center or on the back, turns it on. From there your phone does the rest.

Frequently asked questions

Does Mando TV have anything to do with GTA VI?

No. Mando TV is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two. It only helps you avoid losing game time because you cannot find the TV remote.

Is switching the HDMI input free?

Yes. The inputs list in Mando TV is free, just like turning the TV on and using the arrows, OK and volume. You can also let the console switch the input by itself with HDMI-CEC.

Will it work with my TV?

It works with Samsung, LG, Roku and Android TV sets on the same Wi-Fi network. If yours is another brand, the HDMI-CEC trick can still help.

Can I use it to play GTA VI (GTA 6) on PS5 or Xbox?

Mando TV does not touch the console or the game. It only gets the TV turned on and on the right HDMI input so you can sit down and play.
