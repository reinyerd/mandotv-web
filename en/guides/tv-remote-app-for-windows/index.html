TV Remote App for Windows PC

Mando TV has a Windows app that turns your PC or laptop into a TV remote over your home network. Type on the TV with your real keyboard, keep a small remote window on top while you work, and control Samsung, LG, Roku, Google TV or Fire TV from the same app.

See the steps Download free

Step by step

  1. Install Mando TV free from Microsoft Store on a Windows 10 or 11 PC.
  2. Make sure the PC and the TV are on the same network, over Wi-Fi or cable through the same router.
  3. Open Mando TV. The TV shows up in the list. Click it.
  4. Accept the prompt on the TV if it asks (LG, Samsung), or type the code it shows (Google TV, Fire TV).
  5. Click the buttons or type a search with your keyboard. Done.

Keyboard shortcuts (Pro)

With Pro, your PC keyboard becomes the remote: arrow keys to move, Enter for OK, Backspace or Esc to go back, plus and minus for volume, M to mute and Space to pause.

Mini remote (Pro)

A small remote window that stays on top of other windows, so you can change the volume or pause without leaving what you are doing.

Typing on the TV from the PC

The on-screen keyboard of a TV is slow. In Mando TV you type with your real keyboard and the text goes to the TV search box. That part is free.

More details

For the step-by-step version see how to control your TV from a Windows PC. For phone-based control see use your phone as a TV remote over Wi-Fi.

What is free and what is Pro

The arrows, OK, Back, Home, volume, channels, number keys, keyboard, opening apps, switching inputs and power on or off are free. The touchpad, voice commands, favorite apps, routines and the sleep timer are part of Mando TV Pro, a one-time purchase with no subscription. On Windows, Pro is bought through Microsoft Store.

Voice commands from the PC (Pro)

With Pro you can talk to the TV through the PC microphone. The voice is recognized on the PC itself: it needs no internet, no Windows settings to change and works in the 6 languages of the app. What you say never leaves the PC and is not stored. Only on very old processors does the app fall back to Windows voice typing, which needs "Online speech recognition" on in Settings > Privacy & security > Speech and your language added in Windows.

Frequently asked questions

Does the TV remote app for Windows need Bluetooth or a cable?

No. It uses your home network. The PC and the TV only have to be on the same router.

Which TVs can I control from Windows?

Samsung Tizen TVs, LG webOS TVs, Roku TV and players, Google TV and Android TV, and Fire TV.

Can I use my PC speakers or my screen to see the TV?

No. Mando TV is only a remote. It does not mirror or stream the TV picture to the computer.

Why does voice not work on my Windows PC?

Voice is a Pro feature, so check that Pro is active and that the PC microphone works. On most PCs the voice is recognized on the PC itself, with no internet. Only on very old processors does the app use Windows voice typing: then turn on "Online speech recognition" in Settings > Privacy & security > Speech and add your language in Settings > Time & language.
