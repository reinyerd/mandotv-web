Ready in a minute

You do not need to create an account or install anything on the TV.

  1. Same Wi-Fi network

    Your phone and the TV have to be on the same network at home.

  2. Tap your TV

    Mando TV finds it on its own. If it does not show up, you type its IP address.

  3. Accept on the screen

    The first time, the TV asks for permission. You accept once and it is saved.

Quick questions

Which TVs does Mando TV work with?

With Samsung with Tizen (2016 and newer), LG with webOS, Roku (TVs and players) and Google TV or Android TV (Sony, TCL, Philips, Xiaomi, Chromecast with Google TV and others).

Do I need internet to use the remote?

Only your home Wi-Fi network. Mando TV talks directly to the TV, without going through the internet.

Can Mando TV turn on the TV?

Yes, over your home network, if the TV has power-on over the network enabled. Each brand page says where to turn it on, for example LG and Samsung.

Is Mando TV free?

Yes. The TV remote is free and has no ads: buttons, volume, channels, phone keyboard, power-on over the network and Easy mode. Pro is an optional one-time purchase, no subscription, and adds touchpad, apps, inputs, voice, routines and more.

Where can I download it?

It is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Microsoft Store for Windows. On Android you can try it now with the early access on Google Play.
