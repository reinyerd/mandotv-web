Everything on the remote, and more

What you do with your regular remote, plus the things that are a pain with it: typing, opening apps, switching inputs without hunting for buttons and moving fast through menus. Anything marked Pro is a one-time purchase.

  • Big, clear buttonsD-pad, OK, Back, Home, volume, channels and mute, with their names always visible.
  • Touchpad (Pro)Swipe your finger to move through the menus, or use the pointer on TVs that have one.
  • Phone keyboardType a Wi-Fi password or search in apps without picking letter by letter. In YouTube, the search goes straight to the TV.
  • Open your appsNetflix, YouTube and the other TV apps, with one tap.
  • Inputs and soundSwitch to HDMI, live TV or your console. With Pro, on LG you choose whether the TV or the soundbar plays.
  • Turn on the TVIf it is off, Mando TV turns it on over the network and connects by itself.
  • Your wayLight or dark and vibration are free. With Pro, the background color you like, pure black and the classic remote.
  • Share a remote with your familyBuild a simple remote for your TV and send it as a link, for example by WhatsApp. The person who gets it opens it in Mando TV and saves it; the first time, the TV asks for permission for their phone and they accept it on the TV screen. The link carries no key or password of the TV. The guest remote is free and has an expiry date, up to 31 days. Creating the easy remote, with up to 6 giant buttons, is Pro; using it is free.
  • Mirror your screenOn iPhone and iPad, Mando TV walks you to Screen Mirroring in Control Center to show your screen on a TV with AirPlay. Free. Coming soon to Android and Windows.
  • Without opening the app (Pro)Widgets on the iPhone Home Screen and Lock Screen, Control Center buttons and Siri and Shortcuts phrases like "Mute the TV with Mando". In beta. Coming soon to Android too, as a Home screen widget.

Ready in a minute

You do not need to create an account or install anything on the TV.

  1. Same Wi-Fi network

    Your phone and the TV have to be on the same network at home.

  2. Tap your TV

    Mando TV finds it on its own. If it does not show up, you type its IP address.

  3. Accept on the screen

    The first time, the TV asks for permission. You accept once and it is saved.

Wi-Fi, infrared and Bluetooth

Mando TV connects over Wi-Fi. If your Android phone has an infrared blaster, it can also control TVs that are not smart TVs or were never connected to Wi-Fi. On Android 9 or newer, Bluetooth lets the phone work as a Bluetooth keyboard and remote with no Wi-Fi, in beta.

  • AvailableWi-Fi

    Samsung, LG, Roku, Google TV and Android TV, from your phone or computer. Your phone and TV just need to be on the same network.

  • NewInfrared

    On Android phones with an infrared blaster, common on Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO. It works with older TVs or TVs without internet, and it can turn on a TV you never set up: just point the phone at it. It does not appear on iPhone, Windows or Android phones without a blaster.

  • BetaBluetooth

    On Android 9 or newer, free. The phone connects to the TV as a Bluetooth keyboard and remote, with no Wi-Fi needed. It works with Google TV, Android TV and Fire TV; other brands that accept Bluetooth keyboards may work. Roku does not accept them. Not on iPhone or Windows.

Quick questions

Which TVs does Mando TV work with?

With Samsung with Tizen (2016 and newer), LG with webOS, Roku (TVs and players) and Google TV or Android TV (Sony, Philips, TCL, Hisense, Sharp, Xiaomi, Toshiba, Panasonic, Haier, Skyworth, Chromecast with Google TV and others). Support for older TVs is new and in beta: LG NetCast (2011 to 2014), Vizio SmartCast (2016 and newer), Sony Bravia (2013 to 2015, without Android), Panasonic Viera (2011 to 2018) and Philips (2011 to 2015, without Android). Apple TV is also supported, in beta: Mando TV controls it over your home Wi-Fi and pairs with a PIN that the Apple TV shows on screen. Mando TV wakes the Apple TV over Wi-Fi, and if HDMI-CEC is enabled in the Apple TV settings, the TV turns on too.

Do I need internet to use the remote?

Only your home Wi-Fi network. Mando TV talks directly to the TV, without going through the internet.

Does it work over infrared or Bluetooth?

Over Wi-Fi for smart TVs, and over infrared on Android phones that have an infrared blaster (common on Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO), even for TVs that are not smart TVs. Bluetooth is available in beta, for free, on Android 9 or newer: the phone connects to the TV as a Bluetooth keyboard and remote, with no Wi-Fi needed. It works with Google TV, Android TV and Fire TV; other brands that accept Bluetooth keyboards may work, but Roku does not accept them. It is not available on iPhone or Windows.

Can Mando TV turn on the TV?

Yes, over your home network, if the TV has power-on over the network enabled. Each brand page says where to turn it on, for example LG and Samsung.

Is Mando TV free?

Yes. The TV remote is free and has no ads: buttons, volume, channels, phone keyboard, opening apps, switching inputs, power-on over the network, Easy mode and a guest remote with an expiry date. Pro is an optional one-time purchase, no subscription, and adds touchpad, voice, routines, the easy remote to share, quick access and more.

Where can I download it?

It is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Microsoft Store for Windows. On Android you can try it now with the early access on Google Play.

Which languages is Mando TV in?

The app is in 30 languages, including English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Hebrew, Turkish and Hindi. This website is in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian. Voice commands (Pro) are understood in 29 of them.
