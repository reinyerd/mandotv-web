Help

The most common questions, in step order. If you do not find yours, write to us at hola@mandotv.app and we will reply.

Finding the TV

My TV does not show up in the list
  1. Check that your phone and the TV are on the same Wi-Fi network. If your router has a 2.4 GHz network and a 5 GHz network with different names, they sometimes cannot see each other.
  2. Make sure the TV is on.
  3. On an iPhone, check that Mando TV has the "Local Network" permission in Settings, Mando TV.
  4. Tap the "TV not showing up?" button in the TV list: the app checks the most common causes step by step (same Wi-Fi, VPN, local network permission and more) and gives you a "Check again" button after each fix.
  5. If it still does not show up, tap the + button, choose the brand and type the IP address of the TV. You can see it in the network menu of the TV (for example, 192.168.1.50).
Does it work with mobile data or away from home?

No. Mando TV talks to the TV inside your home network, so your phone has to be connected to that Wi-Fi.

Connecting for the first time

Full guides by brand: LG, Samsung, Roku and Android TV.

LG

When you tap the TV, a prompt appears on the TV screen asking for permission. Accept it with your usual remote. You only do this once.

Samsung

The TV asks if you allow the Mando TV connection. Choose Allow with the remote. If you denied it by mistake, go to Settings, General, External Device Manager, Device Connection Manager, and change it to allowed.

Roku

Roku does not ask for permission. If it does not connect, on the Roku go to Settings, System, Advanced system settings, Control by mobile apps, and leave network access on Default or Permissive (on newer Roku software, set Control by mobile apps to Enabled).

Android TV and Google TV

The TV shows a 6-character code. Type it into Mando TV exactly as it appears. It works for Sony, Philips, TCL, Hisense, Sharp, Xiaomi, Chromecast with Google TV and others with Android TV.

Turning on the TV from the app

Mando TV turns on the TV over your home network. Two things are needed for that: you have connected at least once with the TV on, and the TV has power-on over the network turned on. The name of the setting changes by model; these are the most common:

LG

Look for "TV On With Mobile" (or "Turn on via Wi-Fi") in Settings, General, Devices, External Devices (on older models Connection, Mobile Connection Management), and turn it on.

Samsung

Turn on "Power On with Mobile" in Settings, General, Network, Expert Settings.

Roku

On Roku TV sets, turn on "Fast TV start" in Settings, System, Power. Roku players connected to a TV cannot be turned on this way.

Android TV and Google TV

It depends on the manufacturer. Look for "Wake on LAN", "Turn on over network" or "Remote start" in the network or power settings. Some models do not have it.

If something fails

It disconnects or does not respond
  1. On the remote screen, tap Reconnect.
  2. If that does not work, close Mando TV and open it again.
  3. If the TV changed its IP address, remove it from the list and add it again.
How to send us an error report
  1. In Mando TV, tap the Customize button (the sliders at the top).
  2. Tap Copy test report.
  3. Paste it into an email to hola@mandotv.app together with the brand and model of your TV.

The report does not include passwords, codes or anything you type.

I bought Pro and it does not show up

In Customize, tap Restore purchases, with the same store account you bought with. If it still does not show up, write to us.
