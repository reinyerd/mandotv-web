Mando TV / LG

LG TV Remote App for Your Phone

Mando TV is an LG TV remote app: it turns your phone or computer into the remote for your LG smart TV with webOS, over your home Wi-Fi. It helps if you lost the remote, if its batteries died, or if you just want to type faster.

iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and Windows. Free download, no ads. Pro is a one-time purchase, no subscription.

Compatible TVs

LG smart TVs running webOS (most LG smart TV models since 2014). Works with LED, NanoCell, QNED and OLED TVs. Older LG smart TVs with NetCast (2011 to 2014, before webOS) have new beta support: they pair with the 6-digit key shown on the TV.

What you can do with your LG

  • All the buttonsD-pad, OK, Back, Home, volume, channels, mute and playback.
  • Pointer like the Magic Remote (Pro)Move the arrow around the screen with your finger, just like with the LG Magic Remote.
  • KeyboardType in YouTube, Netflix or the TV browser with your phone keyboard.
  • Apps and inputsOpen your apps and switch to HDMI, live TV, AirPlay or Screen Share.
  • Audio output (Pro)Switch between the TV speakers and your soundbar.
  • Turn on the TVIf it is off, Mando TV turns it on over the network and connects by itself.

How to connect your LG to Mando TV

  1. Connect your phone and the TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open Mando TV: your LG TV shows up in the list on its own. Tap it.
  3. A prompt appears on the TV screen asking for permission. Accept it with your usual remote (first time only).

Turn on your LG from your phone

Turn on "TV On With Mobile" (on some models it is called "Turn on via Wi-Fi"). You will find it under Settings, General, Devices, External Devices (on older models Connection, Mobile Connection Management). After that, when you tap the TV in Mando TV while it is off, the app asks if you want to turn it on and connects by itself.

LG questions

Do I need the Magic Remote to use Mando TV?

Only the first time, to accept the permission prompt on the screen. After that you can use just your phone.

Does Mando TV replace LG ThinQ?

To control the TV, yes: you do not need an LG account or to register the TV. Mando TV connects directly over your Wi-Fi.

Can I turn on my LG TV from my phone?

Yes, if you turn on "TV On With Mobile" on the TV and you connected once while the TV was on.

Does it work with older LG TVs from before webOS?

In beta, yes, with LG NetCast smart TVs from 2011 to 2014. They pair with the 6-digit key shown on the TV. It is new, so it can still have rough edges.
